# Reporting Security Issues
The XingGraph team takes security issues seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

To report a security issue, email [info@xing-kj.com](mailto:info@xing-kj.com) and include the word "SECURITY" in the subject line.

We'll endeavor to respond quickly, and will keep you updated throughout the process.
